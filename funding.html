Ajan Ahmed, PhD

Electrical & Computer Engineering · Voice Biometrics & Trustworthy AI

Dr. Ahmed holds a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Clarkson University, an M.Eng. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Southern Illinois University Carbondale, and a B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from North South University, Bangladesh. His research makes voice biometrics reliable in the real world — speaker recognition, biometric quality assessment and standardization, deepfake audio, and trustworthy AI. Based in Edmonton, Alberta.

Funded Projects

  • NSF ERI (Award #2501916): "ERI: Advancing Biometric Identification for Younger Children through Age-Adaptive Technology Innovations". National Science Foundation, Engineering Research Initiation program; July 2025 – June 2027; total $200,000. PI: Dr. Masudul H. Imtiaz (Clarkson University). Role: co-wrote the proposal; graduate researcher on the funded project. Scope: age-adaptive biometric identification for infants and toddlers (ages 0–4) across face, fingerprint, iris (and voice): quality-aware capture devices, longitudinal studies of how biometric traits change in early childhood, and privacy-compliant protocols with healthcare and education partners. Outputs I contributed to: children's longitudinal speaker verification study (IEEE TBIOM, under review); synthetic fingerprints for children under four (IEEE UEMCON 2026); VQI (IEEE Access, 2026) acknowledges this grant.
  • FBI CJIS (via West Virginia University): "Quality Checking and Enrolling High-Quality Voice Samples to Ensure Accessibility and Usability of Data". FBI Criminal Justice Information Services Division, subaward through WVU; Spring 2024 – Spring 2025; total $108,000. PI: Dr. Masudul Imtiaz (Clarkson). Role: co-wrote the proposal; graduate researcher. Outputs: voice quality assessment research that led to the Voice Quality Index (VQI; IEEE Access, 2026) and the speech-quality metrics study (Signals, 2026).
  • NSF CITeR 25S-04C: "Longitudinal Progression of Biometric Recognition in Fingerprint, Face, Iris, and Voice". Team: Dr. S. Schuckers, Dr. M. Imtiaz, M. R. Sumi, N. Venkataswamy, A. Ahmed, L. Igene, A. Hossain (Clarkson); progress poster Fall 2025, final report Spring 2026 (ORNL, Oak Ridge, TN). Center for Identification Technology Research (NSF IUCRC); Fall 2025 – Spring 2026; total $45,000. Co-PI: Dr. Masudul Imtiaz. Role: co-wrote the proposal; graduate researcher; data collector for all modalities; led the voice data analysis. Outputs: children's longitudinal speaker verification study (292 children, 9 years; IEEE TBIOM, under review).
  • NSF CITeR 24F-02C: "Development of Extended-Length Audio Dataset for Advanced Deepfake Synthesis and Detection". Spring 2025 – Fall 2025; total $45,000. PI: Dr. M. Imtiaz; team: R. Vijaykumar, A. Ahmed (Clarkson). Role: co-wrote the proposal; student co-investigator; led data curation; presented the progress-report poster to sponsors (CITeR Spring 2025 Program Review, Idiap, Switzerland). Sponsor reporting: 1-page proposal and new-proposal presentation (CITeR Fall 2024, Niagara Falls, NY, Oct 2024); progress poster (Spring 2025, Idiap, Switzerland); final report (Fall 2025, JHU Applied Physics Laboratory, Laurel, MD, Nov 2025). Outputs: ELAD-SVDSR dataset (IEEE DataPort) and IEEE Data Descriptions paper (2026).
  • NSF CITeR 23F-03C: "Evaluating and Progressing the State of the Art in Deepfake Audio Generation and Detection". Funding agency: U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS), through CITeR; Spring 2024 – Fall 2024; total $45,000. PI: Dr. M. Imtiaz; team: Y. Sukhdeve, A. Ahmed, R. Vijaykumar (Clarkson). Role: co-wrote the proposal; student researcher; invited speaker at the Fall 2025 CITeR Webinar. Sponsor reporting: final report presentation (CITeR Fall 2024 Phase II, Niagara Falls, NY, Oct 29, 2024). Outputs: deepfake-ready multi-microphone voice data, deepfake generation and evaluation pipeline, SPEAD dataset.
  • NSF CITeR 23S-07C: "Modified EKF-ResNet Architecture for Automatic Speaker Recognition from Noisy Environment". NSF CITeR; total $45,000. PI: Dr. M. Imtiaz; team: A. Ahmed (lead student researcher), S. Purnapatra (Clarkson). Role: co-wrote the proposal; led the project; invited talk at the International Martigny Biometrics Workshop 2025 (EAB / CITeR / Idiap), Switzerland. Sponsor reporting: presented the final report (CITeR Spring 2024 Program Review, WVU, Morgantown, WV, May 16, 2024). Outputs: noisy-environment adult voice data (VPQAD), denoising pipeline, groundwork for VQI.

  • NSF CITeR 25S-01CW-J: "A Multi-Layered Framework for Robust Deepfake Audio Detection & Tamper Analysis" (funded from Spring 2025); total $45,000. Multi-university project: Dr. M. Imtiaz and A.
