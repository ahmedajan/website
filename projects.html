Data Collections

Summary: 6 human-subjects data collections totaling 500+ participants, including a 9-year multimodal longitudinal child biometrics collection in schools; 4 of the resulting datasets are public on IEEE DataPort.

  • DC1. Clarkson Longitudinal Child Biometrics Collection (school-based, multimodal)
    ongoing since 2016 (CITeR / NSF study); my involvement 2024 – present (every semester), ~7 school visits per semester across 3 schools (elementary, middle, and high school); 35+ school visits so far (Spring 2024 – Fall 2026)

  • DC2. VSEA-DC: Voice Sample Enrollment Analysis Data Collection (FBI; NDA)
    100 adults · 6 real-world environments · 2 platforms · 598 baseline recordings (~40–50 h) · project lead; wrote the IRB protocol. See PR3.

  • DC3. VPQAD: Voice Pre-Processing and Quality Assessment Dataset
    50 adults (ages 18–40) · cafeteria and engineering lab · dual microphone (AT2020 + SM58) · text-dependent and text-independent · public on IEEE DataPort · lead. See PR9, J7.

  • DC4. ELAD-SVDSR: Extended-Length Audio Dataset for Synthetic Voice Detection and Speaker Recognition
    Pilot 8 subjects × 3 × 15 min, then 36 participants × 45 min · 5 synchronized microphones · deepfakes included · public on IEEE DataPort · curated the collection and wrote the paper. See PR5, J5.

  • DC5. CUADS: Clarkson University Affective Research Dataset
    38 participants · ECG / PPG / GSR (Shimmer3) · 20 emotion-eliciting film clips · Big Five personality · public on IEEE DataPort · co-designed the protocol, recruited participants, supervised all sessions. See PR10, J6.

  • DC6. SPEAD: Speech Phoneme-Enriched Audio Dataset
    100 speakers (+18 low-phoneme-density controls) · narrations, dialogues, tongue twisters · public on IEEE DataPort · co-creator (mentee-led). See PR16.
